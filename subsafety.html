Safety & Complience

HSE Objectives & Targets

  1. 1. Every task we perform is guided by rigorous risk assessment - from routine job safety analyses to formal reviews before new projects or equipment changes. High-risk activities are governed by strict Permit to Work controls, and our teams conduct monthly cross-departmental Hazard Hunts to catch risks before they become incidents.

  2. 2. Safety starts with knowledge. Every employee, contractor, and visitor completes HSE induction before site access, and our workforce maintains 98%+ on-time completion of safety certifications. Weekly Toolbox Talks keep teams aware of the risks specific to their daily tasks.

  3. 3. We're shifting from reacting to incidents to predicting and preventing them. Every employee contributes at least 3 safety observations a year, near misses are investigated within 48 hours, and corrective actions are closed at a 95%+ rate. Our goal: no hazard should ever be found by an external auditor before we've found it ourselves.

  4. 4. Safety commitment starts at the top. Senior managers conduct monthly site safety walkthroughs, lead Toolbox Talks, and participate in monthly HSE Committee Meetings - and HSE performance is now built into every employee's annual appraisal.

  5. 5. Safety is everyone's responsibility. Each employee reports at least 3 safety observations or near misses per year, complies fully with our Golden Rules and PPE requirements, and completes assigned training on time - with quarterly feedback on personal HSE performance.
